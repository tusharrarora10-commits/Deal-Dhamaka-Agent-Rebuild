# Paid receipt history and contact export
Paid entries marked together are stored with a shared paymentBatchId and shown as one receipt. The Payment tab lists individual payment batches newest first, with brand-wise amounts, date/time, brand count, gross, Campaign support Charges and net paid. Each receipt has its own share action; the newest is labelled Share latest receipt. Receipt image and WhatsApp text use the paid timestamp.
Older paid rows without batch IDs are grouped by contact and paid minute as a best-effort legacy grouping. Newly marked payments use explicit batch IDs. Contacts > Export Excel exports every field present in each saved contact object, including name, WhatsApp number, UPI ID, category and Amazon profile link. If XLSX is unavailable, CSV is used as fallback.
